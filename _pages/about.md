I am currently pursuing a **Ph.D. in Computer Science** at **Virginia Tech**, where I am advised by
[Dr. Brendan David-John](https://cs.vt.edu/people/faculty/brendan-david-john.html)
and [Dr. Bo Ji](https://people.cs.vt.edu/boji/). I am affiliated with the
[PrivateEye Lab](https://bmdj-vt.github.io/), the
[Synergistic Networking and Augmented Intelligence Lab (SNAIL)](https://people.cs.vt.edu/boji/group.html),
and Virginia Tech's [Center for Human-Computer Interaction (CHCI)](https://hci.icat.vt.edu/).

My areas of research interests include **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)**, **Usable Security and Privacy (S&P)**, **AI Alignment and Evaluation**, and **Extended Reality (XR)**. My current work brings these areas together to explore how emerging technologies can become more intelligent, adaptive, and responsive to human needs, particularly around privacy and security. Broadly, my research is guided by four questions: (1) How can we evaluate whether AI technologies that power intelligent systems operate in ways that align with human values and expectations? (2) How can intelligent systems be designed to adapt their behavior as their surrounding context changes? (3) How should people interact with intelligent systems to support meaningful and human-centered interaction? (4) How can intelligent systems be systematically benchmarked in ways that are reproducible, comparable, and device-agnostic? If any of these questions resonate with your work, feel free to reach out. I am always happy to discuss ideas and potential collaborations!
