I am currently pursuing a **Ph.D. in Computer Science** at [Virginia Tech](https://cs.vt.edu/), where I am advised by
[Dr. Brendan David-John](https://cs.vt.edu/people/faculty/brendan-david-john.html)
and [Dr. Bo Ji](https://people.cs.vt.edu/boji/). I am affiliated with the
[PrivateEye Lab](https://bmdj-vt.github.io/), the
[Synergistic Networking and Augmented Intelligence Lab (SNAIL)](https://people.cs.vt.edu/boji/group.html),
and Virginia Tech's [Center for Human-Computer Interaction (CHCI)](https://hci.icat.vt.edu/).

My current research lies at the intersection of **human-computer interaction (HCI)**, **usable security and privacy (S&P)**, **applied artificial intelligence (AI)**, and **extended reality (XR)**. My current work builds toward a broader research agenda on making **emerging technologies intelligent enough to adapt to human needs and values**, particularly around **privacy and security**, while remaining trustworthy, usable, and beneficial in practice. To this end, I study: (1) **standardized and reproducible XR privacy evaluation**, developing methods and tools that make privacy interventions easier to systematically evaluate, compare, and improve across devices; (2) **privacy indicator design**, creating and evaluating multimodal, context-aware interventions that make users and situationally impaired bystanders aware of sensitive data collection; and (3) **XR security**, investigating how people experience and respond to attacks in immersive collaborative environments. 

**I approach research as a cross-domain problem solver**: identifying consequential human problems, developing technical or interaction-level interventions, rigorously evaluating them, and using those findings to improve human experience with technology.

I also earned my **M.S. in Computer Science** from **Virginia Tech**. Before joining Virginia Tech, I completed my
**B.S. in Computer Science** with a **minor in Psychology** at the **Lahore University of Management Sciences (LUMS)**.
At LUMS, I conducted undergraduate research in the
[Technology for People Initiative Lab (TPI)](https://tpi.lums.edu.pk/) under the supervision of
[Dr. Muhammad Fareed Zaffar](https://lums.edu.pk/lums_employee/422) and
[Dr. Murtaza Taj](https://lums.edu.pk/lums_employee/2860).
