## Abstract

Zaamin was conceptualized as an Android application that organizes legal, health, and academic-scholarship information for Pakistani women. The project followed the full UX cycle—from formative research and ideation through high-fidelity prototyping—and evaluated the design through usability testing with 15 participants.
