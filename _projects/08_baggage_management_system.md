**Technologies:** Web3.js, Solidity, Node.js, Express, React/Redux, Prisma, PostgreSQL

## Abstract

This prototype linked baggage barcodes with airline ticket numbers and recorded location updates on Ethereum to provide an immutable history of luggage movement. The backend integrated barcode scanning, blockchain writes, and a web interface, with location updates designed to appear in under one minute.
