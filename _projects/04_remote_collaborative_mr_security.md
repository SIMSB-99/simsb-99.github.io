**Publication:** “Just stop doing everything for now!”: Understanding Security Attacks in Remote Collaborative Mixed Reality  
**Venue:** IEEE VR 2025  
**Device:** Microsoft HoloLens 2

## Abstract

Remote collaborative mixed reality introduces attacks that exploit three-dimensional interaction and invisible virtual geometry. This project implemented latency, click-redirection, object-occlusion, and spatial-occlusion attacks in a collaborative HoloLens 2 environment and studied how users recognized, interpreted, and responded to them. Participants were generally less likely to recognize immersive attacks that exploited MR-specific interaction mechanics than attacks resembling familiar desktop-security failures. The findings identify a gap in user awareness and motivate clearer system feedback, training, and mitigations for maintaining trust in remote immersive collaboration.
