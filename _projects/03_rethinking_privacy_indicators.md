title: Rethinking Privacy Indicators in Extended Reality
abstract: >
  Conventional XR privacy indicators, such as small recording LEDs, assume that nearby bystanders are
  attentive and able to notice visual cues. This project introduces the concept of situationally impaired
  bystanders—people who may be distracted, visually limited by the situation, or otherwise unable to
  perceive a conventional indicator. Four participatory focus groups with eight participants generated
  candidate designs, which were consolidated into five indicators spanning visual, auditory, haptic,
  proximity-, gaze-, and activity-triggered cues. A scenario-based evaluation with seven additional
  participants found that visual-only indicators were often judged less useful in impairment scenarios,
  while adaptable multimodal indicators were preferred in privacy-sensitive contexts.
