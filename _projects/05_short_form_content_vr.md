title: Short-Form Content Consumption in Virtual Reality
abstract: >
  This project investigates how immersive presentation changes the experience of consuming short-form
  media. A within-subjects study compared personalized short-form content in VR and on a smartphone using
  counterbalancing, VR acclimation, a modified Facebook Flow Questionnaire, and perceived-time measures.
  The analysis found higher mean flow and stronger time distortion in VR, suggesting that immersive short-
  form interfaces may intensify engagement and reduce awareness of elapsed time. These results have
  implications for safer, more transparent, and more user-aware immersive media design.
